01 — Who we are

Printing, publishing and design — under one roof.

Unique Printech is a printing and publishing company based in Ahmednagar. We work with businesses, institutions and individuals who need dependable print — from a single visiting card to a full commercial run or a large-format outdoor campaign.

Everything happens in-house: understanding the requirement, DTP and artwork preparation, digital sample printing for approval, CTCP plate making, four-colour offset or digital printing, lamination, binding, cutting, packing and dispatch.

That means fewer handoffs, tighter quality control and a production plan you can actually rely on.

04 — Our process

Nine steps from brief to delivery.

A transparent workflow — you always know which stage your job is in, and what comes next.

  1. 1

    Understand Requirement

    Purpose, quantity, size, paper, deadline and budget — we start by listening.

  2. 2

    DTP / Design

    In-house studio prepares artwork or refines client-supplied files.

  3. 3

    Digital Sample Print

    A proof is produced so you can approve colour, paper and finish.

  4. 4

    Plate Making

    CTCP plates produced in-house for the offset press.

  5. 5

    Final Printing

    Four-colour offset or digital run, based on the job's scope.

  6. 6

    Lamination

    Thermal, cold, Spot UV, texture and pattern options.

  7. 7

    Binding & Cutting

    Perfect binding, saddle, spiral; programmed precision cutting.

  8. 8

    Packing

    Careful packing per order specification for safe transport.

  9. 9

    Ready for Delivery

    Dispatched on schedule — ready for pickup or delivery.
